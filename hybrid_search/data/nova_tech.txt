NovaTech Energy Ltd – Q3 2023 Earnings Report

Revenue for Q3 2023 was $4.82 billion, representing a 12% year-over-year increase. 
Operating income declined to $410 million due to rising lithium and copper costs.

Net income stood at $295 million with earnings per share of $1.18.

Operating margin decreased to 8.5% compared to 10.2% in Q2 2023.

The company reported total debt of $2.1 billion and cash reserves of $740 million.

Management expects Q4 revenue between $4.9 and $5.1 billion with margin improvement.
